# Favourdikewrites

Create a premium, minimalist, professional portfolio website for **Favour Dike**, applying for Turing's Personal Account Rater role.



### Brand Identity

**Title:** AI Response Evaluator | Content Analyst | AI Prompt Specialist



**Tagline:** "Making AI more accurate, relevant, and genuinely helpful."



### Objective

Demonstrate my suitability for evaluating Gemini's use of personal context by showcasing my analytical thinking, English comprehension, AI literacy, attention to detail, contextual reasoning, and ability to provide structured, objective feedback.



### Website Design

Build a modern, elegant, responsive portfolio with:

- Clean white/off-white background, charcoal typography, and subtle electric-blue accents.

- Premium typography, generous whitespace, smooth animations, and excellent mobile responsiveness.

- A professional, technology-inspired aesthetic without generic AI imagery.



### Website Sections



**1. Hero**

Headline: "I Help AI Understand Context, Evaluate Quality, and Deliver Better Responses."



Supporting text: "Combining language expertise, analytical thinking, and practical AI experience to assess relevance, accuracy, helpfulness, and contextual personalization."



CTA: Explore My Evaluation Work | Download CV



**2. About Me**

Present me as a writer, AI prompt specialist, and analytical thinker with experience researching complex topics, developing AI-assisted content, and assessing information quality.



Emphasize critical thinking, clear communication, consistency, and the ability to identify errors, irrelevant information, and unsupported assumptions.



**3. Core Competencies**

Create elegant skill cards for:

- AI Response Evaluation

- Contextual Personalization

- English Language Comprehension

- Critical Thinking & Reasoning

- Accuracy & Instruction Following

- Quality Assurance & Feedback

- AI Prompt Engineering

- Research & Information Analysis



**4. Interactive Evaluation Showcase**

This is the most important section.



Build a realistic, interactive AI response evaluation dashboard featuring fictional scenarios.



Allow visitors to compare AI-generated responses and assess them based on:

- Relevance

- Accuracy

- Helpfulness

- Contextual Understanding

- Personalization



Include sample ratings, explanations, detected errors, and improved responses.



Demonstrate how I identify irrelevant context, unsupported assumptions, factual inconsistencies, and poor instruction-following.



Clearly label all examples as self-directed portfolio demonstrations.



**5. Evaluation Process**

Present a simple five-step visual workflow:



Understand User Intent → Analyze Response → Assess Quality → Identify Errors → Provide Structured Feedback.



**6. Contact**

Include email, (favourdike143@gmail.com) and a professional contact section.



### Important Requirements

- Build a complete, functional website, not a static mockup.

- Make the interactive evaluation dashboard the visual centerpiece.

- Use fictional examples only; never expose personal Gmail or Google Photos information.

- Do not fabricate employment history, certifications, clients, or professional AI evaluation experience. The ai company I've worked with is Micro 1 so you can add the logo of however.

- Make the website recruiter-focused, concise, credible, and visually impressive.

- Prioritize demonstrating my capabilities over simply listing them.



The final result should communicate one clear message: **I have the analytical precision, language skills, and AI familiarity needed to contribute to high-quality AI evaluation.**
